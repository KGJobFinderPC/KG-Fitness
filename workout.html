LEGS

Bodyweight Squat

NOT COMPLETED

A simple bodyweight exercise that trains the legs and glutes while helping you build a strong foundation.

SETS 3
REPS 12
REST 60s

HOW TO PERFORM

Stand with your feet about shoulder-width apart. Keep your chest up, bend your knees and lower your hips. Push through your feet to return to the starting position.

COMMON MISTAKES & SAFETY

Keep your knees aligned with your feet. Do not rush the movement and stop if you experience sharp or unusual pain.

💪
02
CHEST

Push-Ups

NOT COMPLETED

A classic upper-body exercise that trains the chest, shoulders and triceps.

SETS 3
REPS 8–12
REST 60s

HOW TO PERFORM

Place your hands slightly wider than shoulder-width. Keep your body in a straight line and lower your chest toward the floor. Push back up with control.

COMMON MISTAKES & SAFETY

Keep your hips from dropping or lifting excessively. If standard push-ups are too difficult, use an easier variation.

🍑
03
GLUTES

Glute Bridge

NOT COMPLETED

A beginner-friendly movement that focuses on the glutes and helps strengthen the hips.

SETS 3
REPS 12
REST 60s

HOW TO PERFORM

Lie on your back with your knees bent and feet flat on the floor. Lift your hips while squeezing your glutes, then lower back down slowly.

COMMON MISTAKES & SAFETY

Avoid overextending your lower back. Move slowly and keep your core controlled.

🏋️
04
BACK

Dumbbell Row

NOT COMPLETED

A pulling exercise designed to strengthen the back and improve upper-body control.

SETS 3
REPS 10 / ARM
REST 60s

HOW TO PERFORM

Support yourself with one hand and keep your back stable. Pull the dumbbell toward your hip while keeping your elbow close to your body.

COMMON MISTAKES & SAFETY

Avoid twisting your body or using momentum. Choose a weight that allows you to maintain good form throughout the movement.

💪
05
SHOULDERS

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

NOT COMPLETED

A controlled pressing movement that trains the shoulders and triceps.

SETS 3
REPS 10
REST 60s

HOW TO PERFORM

Hold the dumbbells at shoulder level. Press them upward with control and lower them back down without swinging.

COMMON MISTAKES & SAFETY

Do not use excessive weight. Keep your movement controlled and avoid forcing your shoulders into an uncomfortable position.

🧘
06
CORE

Plank

NOT COMPLETED

An isometric core exercise that helps improve stability and body control.

SETS 3
TIME 30 SEC
REST 45s

HOW TO PERFORM

Place your forearms on the floor and extend your legs behind you. Keep your body in a straight line and brace your core while breathing normally.

COMMON MISTAKES & SAFETY

Do not let your lower back sag. Stop the exercise if you cannot maintain proper form.
